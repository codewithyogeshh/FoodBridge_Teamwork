# Food Safety & Thermal Compliance Protocols (FSSAI Aligned)

## 1. Temperature Control Thresholds
- **Cooked Hot Meals**: Must be maintained above **60°C** during transit using insulated thermal bags.
- **Perishable Dairy & Chilled Foods**: Must be transported below **4°C**.
- **Ambient Fresh Produce**: 15°C - 20°C in clean, aerated crates.

## 2. Transit Windows
- **Cooked Meals (Critical Urgency)**: Maximum 3 hours from packaging to distribution.
- **Bakery & Grains**: Up to 48 hours with dry moisture seal.
- **Packaged Items**: Within manufacturer use-by date.

## 3. Two-Factor Chain of Custody
1. **Donor Packaging Check**: Visual inspection and packaging seal.
2. **Volunteer Verification**: Verification of OTP code provided by donor app.
3. **NGO Intake Inspection**: Sensor thermal reading logged before distribution to beneficiaries.
